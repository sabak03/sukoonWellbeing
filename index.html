Why Sukoon?

A safe space for women and families to feel supported

Sukoon Community Wellbeing CIC exists to make wellbeing activities more accessible for families who may feel isolated, face financial barriers, or need a welcoming space to connect with others. Through community-led sessions, we aim to improve confidence, reduce loneliness and support healthier family routines.

Our Impact

Small sessions. Real difference.

Every activity creates an opportunity for families to leave the house, meet others, build confidence and feel part of the community.
